About this prototype

This is a design and motion study. All imagery is generated or illustrated, the residence is fictional, and the floor plan is conceptual. No property, location, specification or availability is represented as factual.

The journey moves from clouds to a palace window and grand hall, through a bedroom and bathroom, into an ornamental detail, and finally from a flat floor plan to an architectural volume. Use the chapter menu to go directly to any scene. Reduced motion is available at the bottom of the experience.
